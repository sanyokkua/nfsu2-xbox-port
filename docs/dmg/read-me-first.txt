NFS Underground 2 for Mac
=========================

You need: an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) with macOS 15 or later, and your own disc image (.iso) of
Need for Speed: Underground 2 for the original Xbox, USA.

1. Drag "NFS Underground 2 Installer" to Applications and open it.
   The first time, macOS blocks it (it isn't signed by Apple): open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
   scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to the installer, then confirm.
2. If macOS asks for Apple's Command Line Tools, click Install. The installer waits and goes on by itself.
3. Choose your disc image. The installer copies the game's files (2.6 GB) to
   ~/Library/Application Support/NFS Underground 2, translates the game's program to C and compiles it on
   your Mac (a few minutes, once).
4. "NFS Underground 2" is put in Applications and opens. Play it from there from now on.
   Settings menu: Back + Start on a controller, F1 (fn+F1 on a MacBook) or Cmd+,. Full screen on/off: F11.

This DMG holds no game code and no game data. Details, credits and the source: https://github.com/sanyokkua/nfsu2-xbox-port
